A sidebar section: an overline title with an italic serif section sign (`§1`, `§2`, ...) in `accent`, then the content, closed by a 1px `rule`. Sections are flat, separated by hairlines, never boxed cards or shadowed tiles.

**Provide** a short title in capitals (set by the overline style) and number the sections in reading order; the last section has no rule below.

- Vertical rhythm: `space-5` above the title, `space-3` between title and content, `space-4` below.
- The wells inside a panel (formulas) are the only boxed things; see FormulaRow.
- Titles are Portuguese noun phrases without a final period.
